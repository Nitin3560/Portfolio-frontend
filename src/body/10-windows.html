Thesis

Thesis

Cross-Layer Supervisory Control for Low-Altitude UAV Swarm Networks

Fall 2026

M.S. Computer Science Thesis · Computer Science and Engineering · UT Arlington

A cross-layer supervisory control framework for low-altitude UAV swarms operating under dynamic and uncertain conditions.

  • Augments a classical PID controller with a lightweight supervisory layer that monitors dynamics, observation, and networking behavior.
  • Identifies likely fault conditions and applies bounded adaptations to reference motion, formation geometry, and coordination parameters.
  • Evaluated in multi-UAV simulation with controlled fault injection and multi-seed experiments, improving robustness under disturbance and communication faults while preserving comparable nominal performance.

Advisors: Dr. Md Salik Parwez, Dr. Debashri Roy, and Dr. Nadra Guizani.
